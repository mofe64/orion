## Reproduction and pitfalls
The final implementation scripts are `print-release/scripts/05_apply_print_repairs.py`, `03b_group_base_reconstruct.py`, `13_cleanup_export_master.py`, `14_final_static_audit.py`, `12_reconcile_master.py`, `15_capture_and_save.py`, and `16_package_handoff.py`. They are stage scripts, not safe to rerun blindly on the completed master. CAD helper is `fusion_client.py`, localhost port27182; a client timeout does not cancel an active Fusion operation.

Fusion imports a two-cap STEP compound as nested temporary components. Copy their native solids with child transforms; do not assume the imported root contains direct bodies. Grouping `moveToComponent` can leave orphan solver constraints: explicitly capture/remove old joints, restore all recorded world positions through ROOT-context occurrence proxies, anchor the printed base first, recreate as-built structural joints before parametric electronics joints. Nested fixed components use isGroundToParent, not native isGrounded. Strict checks rolled failed trials back. Deleting old constraints/recomputing the large supplier history took several minutes per trial; do not interrupt an active rollback.
Historical imported joints can expose an invalid timeline object: require a valid nonnegative timeline index before batch suppression. Remove obsolete imported joints directly; already broken historical joint assets may refuse renaming. All valid relationships and positions must still match after cleanup.
