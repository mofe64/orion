## Reproduction and gotchas
Final stage scripts in `orionV2CAD/head140`:02_geometry.py (shell),05_update_plate.py (replace only head mesh in known-good3MF),11_final_checks.py,07_apply.py,12_static_audit.py,13_reconcile.py,14_save.py,15_publish.py. These are stage scripts, not safe to rerun blindly on the completed master. `08_neck_clearance.py`, `09_check_revised_neck.py` and `rejected-lowered-neck/` are rejected offline trials; never apply them. The prior `motion-access.json` contains genuine pre-relief fork collisions and supports the subset proof; final results are in `final-checks.json`.

Supplier STEP native origins differ from printed head origins: normalize with inverse(shell occurrence transform) times each component transform. This revision's head supplier models need +7 mm Z. Ignoring that creates false horn/servo overlaps and inaccurate CG heights. The final checks correct it. Upper-arm checks use the shared neutral wrist datumZ384 and sampled relative pitch; full Fusion static audit uses world proxies.

Do not remesh unchanged components unnecessarily: remeshing an independent base STEP introduced a one-edge rounding artefact. Instead the released unchanged meshes and3MF objects were reused byte-for-byte. Head mesh was independently closed/manifold checked. The first reconciliation finished the geometry comparison before the concurrent assembly audit completed; its final-stage assertion failed, then passed once the audit was complete.
